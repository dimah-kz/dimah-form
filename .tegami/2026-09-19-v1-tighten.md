### Tighten persist, fill validation, and store CAS

`parseAnswers` drops empty values (blank text, empty multiSelect), not only `null` / `undefined`. After a failed submit, `validate: "change"` keeps `REQUIRED` until the field is filled. `saveForm` re-reads the stored form so the concurrency token matches the adapter. `listForms` pages from the store window so skipped invalid rows do not collapse `nextOffset`. The FumaDB adapter treats a stale CAS write as a conflict even when answers already match.
