### Tighten showWhen visibility

Nested `showWhen` follows the parent field. `equals` and `includes` accept a non-empty scalar list (one-of / any-of). `defineForm` and `saveForm` reject unknown targets, self-references, cycles, extra keys, and `equals` against a `multiSelect`. `$Infer` omits required keys that have `showWhen`. `field(id).required` is true only while the field is visible.
