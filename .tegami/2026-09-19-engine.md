### Engine depth: issues, resume, typed fill

`ValidationIssue` includes stable `code` and optional `params` (`FIELD_ISSUE_CODES`). `startResponse({ resume: true, respondentId })` returns the latest draft for that pair. `validateAnswers` on the instance, client, and fill session runs after per-field validators. `saveForm` honors optional `updatedAt` (`STALE_UPDATE`). Select `$Infer` is a union of option values; `useFormResponse<Form["$Infer"]["answers"][key]>()` types the session. Headless helpers: `emptyToNull`, `formatAnswer`, `formCompletion`.
